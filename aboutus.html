The Journey

Our History

From humble beginnings on coastal docks to an internationally recognized luxury yacht and commercial ship brand.

It All Started From A Dream

Born as the son of a humble carpenter in a quiet coastal town, Everton Deckk spent his childhood watching ships being built and repaired by hand. From a young age, he dreamed of crafting a private yacht—one that wasn’t just luxurious, but deeply personal and full of soul.

The First Boat, A Turning Point

Fueled by years of hands-on experience and a clear vision, Everton finally brought his dream yacht to life—a fully realized vessel that embodied elegance, innovation, and purpose. It wasn’t just a project; it marked the beginning of a new standard in personalized yacht design, capturing the attention of enthusiasts and professionals alike.

The Birth of ShipDecKK

In 2020, Everton turned his vision into a brand—ShipDecKK. More than a yacht company, ShipDecKK was founded to help others design and own personal yachts that represent who they are. With customization at its core, ShipDecKK invites dreamers to co-create vessels that are truly their own.
